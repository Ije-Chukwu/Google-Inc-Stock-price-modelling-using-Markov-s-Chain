# Google-Inc-Stock-price-modelling-using-Markov-s-Chain
Undergraduate project aimed at prediction of Google stock indices ( 2017-2018 ) using Markov Chain Analysis.

Aim and Objectives of the Study

The aim of this study is to model the stock prices of Google company with a
Markov chain. The objectives then include:

1. to analyse a year’s worth of stock portfolio for Google company and apply
moving averages and Markov Chains to the data.
2. to obtain a three day moving average of the stock prices.
3. to obtain the transition probability matrix for the movement of stock prices
from one state to the next.
4. to obtain the steady state behaviour of the stock prices.
5. to predict the stock prices for the near future.
